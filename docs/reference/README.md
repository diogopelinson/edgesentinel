# Reference

Facts to look up while you work: every option, every field, every contract.
Nothing here teaches — the [tutorials](../tutorials/README.md) do that — and
nothing here argues; the reasoning is in
[Explanation](../explanation/README.md).

- **[Configuration](configuration.md)** — every block and field of `config.yaml`, its default, and what the loader refuses.
- **[Command line](cli.md)** — the four commands, their options and their exit codes.
- **[Ports and entities](ports-and-entities.md)** — the contracts in `core/ports.py` and the dataclasses the domain passes around.
- **[Metrics](metrics.md)** — the exact metric names exported by the agent and by the AI Service.
- **[Database](database.md)** — the schema of `events.db`, its indexes, migrations and retention.

The backlog is data too: [roadmap.json](../roadmap.json) holds 48 features with
their dependencies, and its shape is described in [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md).
